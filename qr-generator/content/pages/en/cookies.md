## Before your consent

The site sets **no cookie at all** to work. The generators, the blog and the downloads all function without one. Your language choice comes from the URL, not from a cookie.

Your consent choice itself is stored in your browser's local storage (`consent.ads`) — it never leaves your device and is not sent to our servers.

## After you accept advertising

If you accept, Google AdSense is loaded and may set cookies used to:

- serve ads and limit how often you see the same one;
- measure the performance of a campaign;
- personalise advertising, if you accepted that.

These cookies are set by Google, not by us. Their lifetime and purpose are described in [Google's cookie policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/cookies).

## Changing your mind

Use the **Manage cookies** link at the bottom of any page. You can also clear the site's data from your browser settings, which resets the choice entirely.

## Refusing

If you refuse, no advertising script is loaded and every tool keeps working exactly the same way. Refusing costs you nothing.
