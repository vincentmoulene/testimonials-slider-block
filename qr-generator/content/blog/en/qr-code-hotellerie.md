An arriving guest always asks the same twenty questions: the Wi-Fi code, breakfast times, how the heating works, where the bins go, where to eat tonight. You either answer them one by one, or you display them once and for all.

## The Wi-Fi code: useful from the first minute

It is the first thing a traveller looks for, before even putting the suitcase down. A laminated Wi-Fi code on the desk removes the password dictation — since iOS 11 and Android 10 the phone offers to join straight away.

Use your router's guest network, not the one your computer and cameras are on. And if you change the password, regenerate and reprint the same day: the printed code holds the old one.

## The welcome guide: where a QR code really earns its place

A paper binder is out of date the moment a restaurant closes, and nobody rereads it. A web page behind a QR code updates in thirty seconds, shows in the phone's language, and holds what a binder cannot: a thirty-second video showing how to work the induction hob or close the stubborn shutter.

What belongs in it:

- Wi-Fi, heating, appliances, television
- Check-in and check-out times, waste sorting, departure instructions
- Your recommendations: the three restaurants, the bakery, the market, the late pharmacy
- The number to call when something breaks

## The other codes that earn their place

- **On the door**: a directions code to the exact entrance — not the middle of the village. That is what prevents the 10pm "we can't find it" call.
- **In the room**: room service, spa, the bill.
- **At departure**: the review code, shown the evening before check-out or slipped into the invoice, while the stay went well.
- **On the equipment**: a code on the boiler or the washing machine linking to the manual and the repair company — for you, not for the guest.

## Three common mistakes

1. **A code under glossy plastic, under a spotlight.** Reflections kill the scan. Test it in the evening with the lights on.
2. **A welcome guide as a PDF.** Unreadable on a phone. A plain web page, always.
3. **One code for everything.** Wi-Fi and the welcome guide are two needs at two different moments. Two codes, two clear labels.

## The takeaway

Wi-Fi helps from the first minute; the welcome guide saves you hours across a season and removes half your messages. Start with those two — the rest is a bonus.
